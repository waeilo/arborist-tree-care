Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a municipal permit to remove a tree? expand_more

Permit requirements depend on species classification, diameter at breast height (DBH), and local town bylaws. Our certified team prepares and files all municipal permits for you.

How do you calculate tree removal costs? expand_more

Costs depend on tree height, canopy spread, proximity to structures or wires, and equipment needed (crane vs. climb). We provide exact itemized quotes before beginning work.

Are your crews and trucks fully insured? expand_more

Yes. We carry $10,000,000 in comprehensive commercial general liability and complete workers' compensation insurance. Proof of coverage is sent directly upon request.

Can a diseased tree be saved without cutting it? expand_more

Very often yes! Through sonic tomography, root aeration, and structural support cables, we frequently save trees that others recommend removing. Removal is always our last option.

What happens to the wood and sawdust? expand_more

You can keep the wood cut into fireplace logs or turned into garden mulch. Otherwise, we haul 100% of it away for bio-recycling at zero extra trouble.

Common Questions

Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know about our arboricultural services, permits, and scheduling.

Do I need a municipal permit to remove a tree on my property? expand_more

Permit requirements depend on the tree's diameter at breast height (DBH), species classification, and municipal jurisdiction. Our certified arborists prepare, submit, and manage all necessary municipal tree permits on your behalf.

How do you calculate tree removal or pruning costs? expand_more

Pricing is calculated based on height, canopy volume, species wood density, physical proximity to structures/utility lines, and required equipment (e.g., crane vs. manual rope rigging). We provide binding, itemized proposals.

Are your crews and company fully insured? expand_more

Yes. We carry $10,000,000 in comprehensive commercial liability insurance and full workers' compensation coverage for all field technicians. Certificates of insurance are provided directly from our underwriter upon request.

Can a diseased or dying tree be preserved instead of cut down? expand_more

Often, yes. Through sonic tomography, root flare micro-injections, and dynamic canopy cabling, we frequently save trees that other services recommend felling. Removal is always our last resort.

What happens to the wood and woodchips after service? expand_more

You can keep the wood cut to firewood lengths or chipped into organic mulch for your gardens. Otherwise, we haul 100% of the material to our biomass repurposing partner where it is recycled.
